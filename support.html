Support

Glucose Log is made by one person. Email williamscott701@gmail.com and you will get a reply, usually within two working days.

It helps to say which iOS version you are on and what you were doing at the time. Please do not send readings or health data by email — nothing about your logbook is visible from this end.

Where your data is

Every reading is stored on your device. There is no account and no server, so there is nothing to sign into and nothing to recover remotely. Deleting the app deletes the logbook with it — export first if you want to keep it.

Moving your logbook

  • To a new phone. An encrypted iPhone backup, or iCloud device backup, carries the logbook across.
  • To a spreadsheet. Settings → Export as CSV.
  • To your clinician. The Report tab produces a one-page PDF you can print, email or AirDrop.
  • To Apple Health. Readings you enter are written to Health when you allow it, and existing Health entries can be imported so nothing is lost.

Glucose Log Pro

Logging, charts and the widgets are free and stay free. Pro is one annual subscription that adds the clinician PDF, history beyond 30 days, Health import and CSV export. It renews yearly until cancelled.

  • Restore a purchase. Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
  • Cancel. iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Glucose Log. Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps access until the period ends.
  • Refunds. Apple handles these, not us, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What this app is not

Glucose Log is a logbook. It is not a medical device, it does not connect to a meter or CGM, and it never recommends a dose or a treatment. The estimated A1c it shows is a published regression over the numbers you typed in, not a laboratory result. Decisions about your treatment belong with your clinician.

If something in the app is wrong, say so in that email. A logbook that shows the wrong number is worth fixing quickly.
