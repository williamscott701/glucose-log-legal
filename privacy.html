Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026

Glucose Log is a logbook that runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no server and no analytics. This policy describes what the app does with your information, which is very little.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your device:

  • Blood glucose readings, with the date and time you recorded them.
  • Optional carbohydrate amounts, insulin doses and notes.
  • The context you tag a reading with, such as fasting or after a meal.
  • Your settings: units, target range, reminder time, and the name you choose to

print on reports.

This is held in the app's own storage on your device and in a shared container that lets the widgets read it. It is not uploaded anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for your email address.
  • It does not send your readings to us or to anyone else.
  • It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Glucose Log writes the readings you enter to Apple Health, and can read blood glucose readings you already have there so your logbook is complete. It asks for no other Health data.

Health data is handled by Apple's HealthKit on your device. Glucose Log never transmits it, and never uses Health data for advertising or marketing. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app, under Sharing.

Reports and exports

Creating a PDF report or a CSV export writes a file to a temporary folder on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is entirely your choice — mail, Files, a printer, another app. The app itself sends the file nowhere.

Subscriptions

Glucose Log Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app only ever sees whether a subscription is currently active. We never receive your payment details.

Purchases are managed in your Apple Account settings, where they can also be cancelled.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server, and no notification contains your readings.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. Delete all readings in Settings erases every reading from the device, and deleting the app removes everything it stored, including your settings. Readings you previously wrote to Apple Health belong to Health and are deleted there.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or delete on your behalf — you already hold all of it.

Children

Glucose Log is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Glucose Log records and displays what you enter. It does not diagnose, does not recommend insulin doses, and is not a substitute for laboratory testing or for advice from your clinician. Estimated A1c is an estimate derived from the mean of the readings you have logged, not a laboratory result.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the updated policy ships in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address on the App Store listing for Glucose Log.
