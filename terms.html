What Glucose Log is

Glucose Log is a manual logbook. You type in readings you have already measured yourself, and the app stores them, charts them and can produce a PDF report.

It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, does not recommend or calculate insulin doses, does not connect to any meter or sensor, and is not a substitute for laboratory testing or for advice from your clinician. Estimated A1c is an estimate derived from the readings you entered using a published regression; it is not a laboratory result.

Decisions about your treatment are between you and your clinician. Never change a dose, a medication or a treatment plan on the basis of anything this app displays.
